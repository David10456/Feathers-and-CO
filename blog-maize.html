Maize Cultivation for Poultry Feeding: A Sustainable Approach

  • Feathers and CO Team
  • Aug 12, 2026
  • 3 Comments

At Feathers and CO POULTRY FARM, we believe that high-quality poultry products start with high-quality feed. Maize is the cornerstone of any successful poultry diet, providing the essential carbohydrates and energy required for optimal growth in broilers and consistent egg production in layers. However, relying solely on external suppliers can be costly and inconsistent. That is why we have integrated maize cultivation directly into our agricultural operations here in Nkolafamba, Yaoundé.

Our cultivation process begins with careful soil preparation and the selection of high-yield, drought-resistant maize varieties suited to the Cameroonian climate. By growing our own maize, we maintain complete control over the quality of the grains, ensuring they are free from harmful pesticides and rich in the natural nutrients our birds need to thrive.

"By growing our own maize, we don't just cut operational costs; we guarantee that every single grain fed to our birds is safe, traceable, and packed with the energy they need to grow healthy and strong."

— Feathers and CO Farm Manager

Harvesting is done at the precise moment of peak maturity to maximize nutritional value. Post-harvest handling is equally critical. We employ strict drying protocols to reduce the moisture content of the maize to below 13%, which is the gold standard for preventing aflatoxin contamination, a major threat to poultry health. Our elevated, well-ventilated storage facilities ensure the grain remains dry and pest-free until it is ready for milling.

Best Practices for Maize Storage and Quality Control

Proper storage is where many small-scale farms face challenges, but we have made it a priority. Our storage silos are regularly inspected and treated with safe, approved methods to deter weevils and rodents. Before any batch of maize is sent to our feed mixing station, it undergoes a rigorous quality check. This meticulous attention to detail directly impacts the health of our 1,000 broilers, 20 agric-fowls, and 30 old layers, resulting in better feed conversion rates and healthier birds.

Integrating Maize into Our Feed Formulation

Maize alone is not enough; it must be part of a balanced diet. At Feathers and CO, we combine our home-grown maize with locally sourced soybeans for protein, and innovative supplements like reared maggot meal for additional amino acids. This custom feed formulation is designed specifically for our different flocks, ensuring that our broilers reach market weight efficiently, and our layers produce large, nutrient-rich eggs.

This closed-loop, sustainable approach not only makes our business more resilient to market price fluctuations but also allows us to offer our customers the best possible prices without ever compromising on the quality or safety of our chicken and eggs.
