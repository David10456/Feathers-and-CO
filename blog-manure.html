Organic Manure Production from Poultry Waste: Turning Waste into Wealth

  • Feathers and CO Team
  • Aug 5, 2026
  • 2 Comments

At Feathers and CO POULTRY FARM, we believe that in agriculture, there is no such thing as "waste"—only resources waiting to be repurposed. Our deep-litter poultry housing system naturally mixes bird droppings with carbon-rich bedding materials like wood shavings and dried leaves. Over time, this mixture undergoes a natural composting process, transforming into a highly potent, nutrient-rich organic fertilizer.

Poultry manure is exceptionally high in Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium (NPK), making it one of the most valuable organic fertilizers available. However, raw manure can be too "hot" for direct application and may contain harmful pathogens. That is why we implement a strict, controlled composting protocol to ensure safety and maximum efficacy.

"We don't just raise chickens; we cultivate soil health. Our organic manure is the bridge that connects healthy poultry farming with thriving crop production in our community."

— Feathers and CO Sustainability Lead

The Composting Process: Safety and Quality First

Proper composting is where many small-scale farms face challenges, but we have made it a core operational priority. The manure is piled into windrows and turned regularly to introduce oxygen. We actively monitor the internal temperature, ensuring it reaches and maintains at least 60°C for several days. This thermophilic phase safely eliminates harmful bacteria, parasites, and weed seeds, resulting in a stable, odor-free, and nutrient-dense final product.

Nutritional Value for Crops

Once fully composted and dried, our organic manure is rich in essential macro and micronutrients. Unlike synthetic chemical fertilizers that can degrade soil structure over time, our organic manure improves soil aeration, increases water retention, and promotes healthy microbial activity in the root zone. This leads to stronger, more resilient crops and higher yields for local farmers.

Supporting Local Crop Farmers: A Symbiotic Relationship

The final step in our process is packaging and distribution. Our dried organic manure is bagged in 25kg and 50kg sacks and made available to local crop farmers in the Nkolafamba and greater Yaoundé regions. This creates a beautiful, symbiotic economic loop: we purchase maize and soybeans from these same farmers for our poultry feed, and they purchase our organic manure for their crops. This keeps financial value circulating within our local community, reduces reliance on expensive imported chemical fertilizers, and promotes sustainable agriculture across Cameroon.

Feathers and CO Team Reply

Great question, Amina! Odor control is a major focus for us. We maintain a strict carbon-to-nitrogen ratio by ensuring enough dry bedding (wood shavings) is mixed with the manure.
